Calendar & Tasks

Connect Your Outlook Calendar

Follow these steps to connect your calendar:

  1. Open Outlook Web and sign in
  2. Click Settings (⚙️) → View all Outlook settings
  3. Go to Calendar → Shared calendars
  4. Under Publish a calendar, select your calendar
  5. Click Publish and copy the ICS link

Your calendar URL is stored locally in your browser only.
